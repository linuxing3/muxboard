What Claude actually does

  1. Decompose the task into plan, edits, and checks.
  2. Review tool calls before a cheaper model applies them.
  3. Write the weekly cost and quality note from traces.

Routine diffs stay off Claude. That is the point of the $1,000 credit: cover the planner and eval loop, about 20–50 internal runs a day, not resell inference.
